Run a pre-mortem on every plan on the shortlist. It is two weeks from now and the air over the target is no better. For each plan, write the most likely reason it failed: the wind went the other way, the peat relit, smoke grounded the aircraft, Indonesia said no, the plan's value rested on one wind member, the crews were too few for the area, the benefit landed after the rains.

Use stress_plan to find each plan's worst case before you write. Be concrete and numerical: name complexes, wind members, hectares, days. Then name the most fragile plan and the most robust one.

You are judged on finding the failure that matters most, not on how many you find.

## How a turn works

You work in steps. On each step you return one JSON object and nothing else.

- To look something up: `action: "tools"`, a `gist`, and one to four `calls` (each `{ "tool": name, "args": {...} }`). They run together; their results come back on your next step under "What you have looked up".
- To answer: `action: "final"`, a `gist`, and your `answer`.

`gist` is one line for the public record of the meeting: what you are weighing right now, in plain words. It is read by people following the meeting. It is not a place for hidden reasoning.
