You read one item about the haze in Malaysia and record, as data, what an authority decided in it. The item is an official press statement (Education Ministry, DOE) or a news headline. Most are in Malay.

Return one JSON object matching this schema and nothing else:

{{schema}}

## Is it a statement

`isStatement` is true only when the item reports a decision or instruction by an authority about the haze: a rule tied to the Air Pollutant Index (API, in Malay IPU), schools or institutions closing or reopening, an emergency being declared or ended, work from home, a ban, or official health advice. It is false for a report of readings ("38 kawasan catat IPU tidak sihat"), commentary, fires abroad, or anything outside Malaysia. When it is false, fill the other fields with their empty values: empty strings, empty lists, false, null, `other`.

## The fields

- `issuer`: `moe` Education Ministry (KPM) or a state education department (JPN); `doe` Department of Environment (JAS); `nadma` the disaster agency; `moh` Health Ministry (KKM); `state` a state government or its Chief Minister (Menteri Besar, Ketua Menteri); `ruler` a Ruler, Regent or the King (Sultan, Pemangku Raja, Yang di-Pertuan Agong) by decree (titah); `federal` the Prime Minister or the federal cabinet; `other` anyone else. Use the authority that made the decision, not the newspaper.
- `issuerName`: as the item names it, in English: "Ministry of Education", "Johor Regent", "JAKIM". Never empty for a statement: when the item names no authority, give the body whose decision it plainly is ("KAFA" classes are JAKIM's; schools are the Education Ministry's).
- `kind`: `rule` when it sets a line that applies wherever it is passed ("schools close where API is above 180"); `order` when it names areas ("all schools in Johor are closed"); `lift` when it ends an earlier order; `advice` for guidance with no order.
- `action`: `schools-close` (schools or education institutions close, or move to learning from home, PdPR); `outdoor-stop` (outdoor activities stop); `emergency` (an emergency is declared); `work-from-home`; `reopen` (schools reopen or an emergency ends); `other`.
- `audience`: who must act, in a word or two: "Schools", "Public", "Civil servants", "Employers".
- `states`: only from this list, spelled exactly so: Johor, Kedah, Kelantan, Melaka, Negeri Sembilan, Pahang, Perak, Perlis, Pulau Pinang, Sabah, Sarawak, Selangor, Terengganu, WP Kuala Lumpur, WP Labuan, WP Putrajaya. Kuala Lumpur is WP Kuala Lumpur; Penang is Pulau Pinang; Malacca is Melaka. List a state only when the whole state is covered.
- `districts`: districts or towns named when only part of a state is covered ("Rompin"). Empty otherwise.
- `nationwide`: true when it covers all of Malaysia, including a rule that applies wherever the line is passed.
- `threshold` and `sustainedDays`: for a rule, the API line and how many days in a row it must hold (null when one reading is enough). Null for anything else. When an item sets more than one rule, record the one that applies first here and name every rule in `summary`.
- `effectiveFrom`: the Malaysian date it starts, `YYYY-MM-DD`. Work out "esok" (tomorrow), "hari ini" (today) and "mulai Isnin" (from Monday) from the publication date and time you are given. A closure announced in the evening "for tomorrow" starts tomorrow. Null when not said.
- `effectiveUntil`: the last day it applies, only when the item states an end or a length: "dua hari" (two days) from 8 October ends on 9 October; "sehingga Isnin" (until Monday) ends on Monday. "Ditutup esok" or "close on Oct 9" states a start and no end: null. Closures usually run until a further notice, so never guess an end.
- `summary`: one plain English sentence under 140 characters saying what was decided, where, from when, without repeating the issuer's name at the start. No opinion, no "reportedly".
- `quote`: the one sentence or headline in the item the decision rests on, copied character for character from the item text, in its own language. Do not translate it, fix it or shorten it in the middle. It is checked against the text, and a quote not found word for word is thrown away.

Record only what the item says. Never add a date, place or line it does not state or plainly imply.
