The Chair has opened the meeting. You draft one plan, alone: you have not seen anyone else's, and you will not until the cross-examination. Draft the best plan you can under **your premise**, from your mandate.

A plan is a list of assignments: which complex, which asset, how many, from which plan day to which. Assets are those in the package; units are aircraft for `cl415` and `helicopters`, teams of six for `ground_teams`, and people for `bomba_domestic` and `ground_task_force`. You may use a line that needs a new Indonesian request: the engine counts it only from the day the request could be granted, and the verdict will show the request as a request. Leave `needsConsent` null; the validator works it out from the place.

Before you answer:

- Look at the complexes that matter to the target (get_complexes, get_influence), not only the biggest or the nearest (R1).
- Match each asset to what it can do (R5, R6): aircraft knock down surface flame near water; only ground crews end peat fires.
- Send enough to finish what you start, or hold its edge (R3, R4).
- Run your plan through simulate_plan, and check_feasibility if unsure. Improve it if the engine shows a better use of the same assets.

In `rationale` say why these complexes, with ids and figures. In `dependsOn` name the one assumption your plan's value rests on. `requests` lists what Indonesia would have to agree to; `share` what Malaysia would hand Indonesia's agency, or null. Keep `headline` to one sentence.

## How a turn works

You work in steps. On each step you return one JSON object and nothing else.

- To look something up: `action: "tools"`, a `gist`, and one to four `calls` (each `{ "tool": name, "args": {...} }`). They run together; their results come back on your next step under "What you have looked up".
- To answer: `action: "final"`, a `gist`, and your `answer`.

`gist` is one line for the public record of the meeting: what you are weighing right now, in plain words. It is read by people following the meeting. It is not a place for hidden reasoning.
