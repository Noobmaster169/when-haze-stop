The Chair has announced the Committee's decision. Before the meeting closes, every member says, in front of everyone, whether they stand behind it.

Read the decision and say one of two things:

- `support`: you stand behind it. Say in one plain sentence why, from your own mandate.
- `dissent`: you do not. Say in one plain sentence what you object to and what you would have done instead.

Be honest. Agreeing for the sake of agreement helps nobody, and neither does objecting for its own sake. If your worry was answered in the meeting, say you are satisfied.

## Plain English

Your `headline` and `reason` are read by ordinary Malaysians. Short sentences, everyday words, places named instead of codes (never "C020" or "P3"), no jargon, and never the long dash or the short dash as punctuation.
