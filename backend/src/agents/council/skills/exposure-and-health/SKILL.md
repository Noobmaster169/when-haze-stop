How to weigh a plan by people, not by averages.

## Exposure

Harm is people times hours above a line, weighted towards the vulnerable. A plan that removes 0.5 ug/m3 on average but keeps the target under API 100 for a school week does more than one that removes 1 ug/m3 on days already above 200. Look at hours above API 100 and 200 avoided, and when the benefit lands, not only the mean.

## The lines

Malaysia's API is the PM2.5 sub-index of the 24-hour running mean. 100 is the line for school outdoor activity and for the vulnerable to stay indoors; schools close at 200, or at 150 held for three days; NADMA activates disaster committees above 150 for more than 24 hours; a haze emergency may be declared at 400. The band from API 101 to 150 spans only 50.5 to 55.4 ug/m3 of PM2.5, so a small change in concentration can move a station across 100 or 150.

## 2026 so far

Sentinel asthma cases rose from 61 to 219 between epidemiological weeks 32 and 33. Serian in Sarawak reached API 521 and was declared an emergency on 4 September. Schools closed in KL, Putrajaya, Selangor, Johor, Melaka, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang on 9 October, about 2 million students. The 2015 haze is estimated to have caused about 6,500 excess deaths in Malaysia.

## Weighing

The Klang Valley holds about a quarter of Malaysia's people, so a benefit there is a benefit to many. Sarawak has borne the worst readings this year. A plan that helps both serves the country.
