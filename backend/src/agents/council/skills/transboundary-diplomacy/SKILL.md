How Malaysian help reaches an Indonesian fire.

## The rules

Under the ASEAN Agreement on Transboundary Haze Pollution (2002; Indonesia ratified in 2014), assistance is given only at the request or with the consent of the receiving state, which directs it (articles 12 and 13). In practice Indonesia's president consents, its foreign ministry processes the note, BNPB runs air operations, and the Forestry ministry runs Manggala Agni and the hotspot data. The ASEAN coordinating centre for haze opened in Jakarta in April 2026 and was not operational this season.

## What has happened

- 1997: about 1,200 Malaysian firefighters worked in Sumatra and Kalimantan; rain, not suppression, ended the haze.
- 2005: 100 Bomba firefighters and 25 SMART went to Riau.
- 2015: Indonesia said help was "not yet needed" on 12 September and accepted it around 8 October; the CL-415 worked from Palembang.
- 2019: Malaysia's offer was declined; Indonesia's environment minister rebuffed Malaysian complaints by pointing at fires in Malaysia and on Malaysian-owned plantations in Indonesia.
- 2023: Jakarta denied its haze reached Malaysia.
- 2026: the NRES minister wrote on 9 September; the Deputy Prime Minister presented the offer in Jakarta on 17 September; President Prabowo accepted it on 18 September, for Kalimantan. On 7 October the ministries were still aligning procedures.

## What that means for a plan

The accepted package may work in Kalimantan. Moving it to Sumatra, or sending ground crews anywhere in Indonesia, is a new request with a lag of days to weeks and real odds of refusal: since 2015 Indonesia has accepted only aircraft (R10). A substitutive request (Sumatra instead of Kalimantan) risks the help already accepted; an additive one does not. Requests framed as joint work land better than demands: Malaysia proposed a science-diplomacy initiative and a haze fund at the August 2026 sub-regional ministerial meeting. A ranking of which fires reach KL, shared agency to agency as joint technical work, is help; the same list handed over as targets reads as direction. Blame for Malaysian-owned concessions is a live sensitivity.
