# What Malaysia can send

The council plans with a stated package. Its first part is what was actually offered and
accepted in September 2026; the rest is assumption, marked as such, with what it rests on. Full
sourcing in `research/02-campaigns-capacity.md`.

## The facts (as of 10 October 2026)

- 9 September: the NRES minister wrote to Indonesia offering help.
- 17 to 18 September: the Deputy Prime Minister, chair of the National Disaster Management
  Committee, presented the offer in Jakarta; President Prabowo accepted it, for Kalimantan.
- The offer: one APMM Bombardier CL-415MP, three helicopters (type not disclosed), 52 personnel
  from NADMA, APMM and TUDM.
- 7 October: the two foreign ministries were still aligning procedures; no report found that the
  team has deployed.
- 8 October: the Prime Minister said he had contacted the President and that Malaysia was ramping
  up cloud seeding (14 operations and over 50 C-130 flights by 30 September).
- 9 October: schools closed in KL, Putrajaya, Selangor, Johor, Melaka, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang.
- For scale: Indonesia's own effort on 20 September was 64 aircraft and 54,394 personnel. Malaysia's
  package is about 6% of the aircraft and 0.1% of the people. Its value depends almost entirely on
  where it is aimed.
